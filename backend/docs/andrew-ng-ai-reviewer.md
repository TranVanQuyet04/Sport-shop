# Andrew Ng va vai tro trong linh vuc AI

## 1. Andrew Ng la ai?

Andrew Ng, ten day du la Andrew Yan-Tak Ng, la mot nha khoa hoc may tinh, giang vien, doanh nhan cong nghe va nha truyen ba tri tue nhan tao. Ong la mot trong nhung nhan vat co anh huong lon trong su phat trien va pho cap cua machine learning va deep learning hien dai.

Andrew Ng duoc biet den nhieu nhat qua cac vai tro:

- Dong sang lap va lanh dao du an Google Brain.
- Dong sang lap nen tang hoc truc tuyen Coursera.
- Sang lap DeepLearning.AI.
- Tung la Chief Scientist tai Baidu.
- Sang lap Landing AI.
- Tham gia xay dung AI Fund.
- Giang day AI va machine learning cho hang trieu nguoi hoc tren toan cau.

Neu noi ngan gon, Andrew Ng la mot trong nhung nguoi giup dua AI tu phong nghien cuu ra dai chung, tu cac bai bao hoc thuat thanh cac khoa hoc, san pham va ung dung thuc te.

## 2. Nen tang hoc van va su nghiep ban dau

Andrew Ng sinh nam 1976. Ong hoc tai nhung truong dai hoc rat manh ve khoa hoc may tinh:

- Carnegie Mellon University.
- Massachusetts Institute of Technology, MIT.
- University of California, Berkeley.

Sau do, ong tro thanh giang vien tai Stanford University, mot trong nhung trung tam nghien cuu AI hang dau the gioi. Tai Stanford, Andrew Ng giang day va nghien cuu ve machine learning, robotics va tri tue nhan tao.

Khoa hoc Machine Learning cua ong tai Stanford va sau nay tren Coursera da tro thanh mot trong nhung khoa hoc noi tieng nhat trong lich su giao duc truc tuyen.

## 3. Vai tro tai Stanford

Tai Stanford, Andrew Ng khong chi la mot nha nghien cuu ma con la mot nguoi thay co anh huong lon. Ong noi tieng voi kha nang giai thich cac khai niem kho trong machine learning mot cach ro rang va co he thong.

Nhung chu de thuong gan voi cac bai giang cua ong gom:

- Linear regression.
- Logistic regression.
- Gradient descent.
- Bias va variance.
- Regularization.
- Neural networks.
- Support vector machines.
- Unsupervised learning.
- Recommender systems.

Dieu lam cac khoa hoc cua Andrew Ng noi bat la cach ong giup nguoi hoc hieu ban chat truoc khi di sau vao cong cu. Vi vay, nhieu nguoi moi bat dau AI thuong duoc khuyen hoc Andrew Ng dau tien.

## 4. Google Brain

Mot dau moc quan trong trong su nghiep cua Andrew Ng la Google Brain.

Google Brain la mot du an nghien cuu deep learning tai Google, duoc xay dung trong giai doan deep learning bat dau bung no. Andrew Ng cung cac nha nghien cuu khac da tham gia thuc day viec huan luyen cac mang neural quy mo lon bang du lieu lon va nang luc tinh toan lon.

Google Brain gop phan lam thay doi cach nhin cua cong nghiep ve deep learning. Truoc do, deep learning van con bi xem la mot huong nghien cuu co nhieu han che. Sau cac thanh cong trong thuc nghiem va ung dung, no tro thanh mot trong nhung huong di chu dao cua AI hien dai.

Vai tro cua Andrew Ng o Google Brain cho thay ong khong chi manh ve giang day, ma con co dong gop trong viec dua deep learning vao moi truong cong nghiep quy mo lon.

## 5. Coursera va giao duc truc tuyen

Nam 2012, Andrew Ng dong sang lap Coursera cung Daphne Koller. Coursera la mot nen tang hoc truc tuyen cho phep nguoi hoc tren toan the gioi tiep can cac khoa hoc tu nhieu truong dai hoc va to chuc lon.

Khoa Machine Learning cua Andrew Ng tren Coursera tro thanh mot hien tuong. No giup rat nhieu sinh vien, lap trinh vien, ky su va nguoi chuyen nganh tiep can AI mot cach co he thong.

Tac dong cua Coursera va cac khoa hoc cua Andrew Ng nam o cho:

- Lam AI tro nen de tiep can hon.
- Giam rao can hoc tap cho nguoi khong o cac truong hang dau.
- Tao nen mot con duong hoc machine learning pho bien cho nguoi moi.
- Gop phan hinh thanh van hoa hoc truc tuyen trong cong nghe.

## 6. Baidu

Sau Google, Andrew Ng tung lam Chief Scientist tai Baidu, mot trong nhung cong ty cong nghe lon cua Trung Quoc. Tai day, ong tham gia xay dung va lanh dao cac nhom nghien cuu AI.

Nhung linh vuc AI lien quan den giai doan nay bao gom:

- Nhan dang giong noi.
- Xu ly ngon ngu tu nhien.
- Thi giac may tinh.
- Deep learning.
- Cac nen tang va san pham AI ung dung.

Giai doan Baidu cho thay Andrew Ng co kha nang dua AI vao cac bai toan cong nghiep lon, khong chi dung lai o giao duc hay nghien cuu hoc thuat.

## 7. DeepLearning.AI

Sau khi roi Baidu, Andrew Ng sang lap DeepLearning.AI. Day la mot to chuc giao duc tap trung vao AI, machine learning, deep learning va gan day la generative AI.

Mot so khoa hoc noi bat cua DeepLearning.AI gom:

- Machine Learning Specialization.
- Deep Learning Specialization.
- AI for Everyone.
- Generative AI for Everyone.
- Machine Learning Engineering for Production.
- Cac khoa ngan ve prompt engineering, large language models, retrieval augmented generation, evaluation va MLOps.

DeepLearning.AI tiep tuc the hien muc tieu lon cua Andrew Ng: dan chu hoa giao duc AI, giup nhieu nguoi hon co the hieu va ung dung AI.

## 8. Landing AI va data-centric AI

Andrew Ng cung la nguoi sang lap Landing AI, mot cong ty tap trung vao viec dua AI vao doanh nghiep, dac biet trong linh vuc thi giac may tinh va san xuat.

Mot khai niem rat gan voi Andrew Ng trong giai doan nay la data-centric AI.

Data-centric AI nhan manh rang de cai thien he thong AI, khong nen chi tap trung vao viec thay doi model. Trong nhieu truong hop, viec cai thien du lieu con quan trong hon:

- Du lieu can sach hon.
- Nhan du lieu can nhat quan hon.
- Quy trinh thu thap du lieu can tot hon.
- Tap train, validation va test can duoc thiet ke dung hon.
- Loi cua model can duoc phan tich de cai thien du lieu.

Tu duy nay rat thuc te trong moi truong doanh nghiep, noi du lieu thuong khong gon gang nhu cac bo du lieu mau trong sach giao khoa.

## 9. AI Fund

AI Fund la mot to chuc dau tu va xay dung startup AI co lien quan den Andrew Ng. Muc tieu cua AI Fund la tim cac co hoi ung dung AI vao nhung bai toan thuc te va xay dung cong ty tu cac co hoi do.

Dieu nay phan anh cach Andrew Ng nhin ve AI: AI khong chi la mot nganh nghien cuu, ma la mot nen tang co the tao ra san pham, doanh nghiep va gia tri kinh te moi.

## 10. Andrew Ng co phai "AI reviewer" khong?

Cum tu "Andrew Ng AI reviewer" co the duoc hieu theo nhieu cach.

Neu "reviewer" nghia la nguoi phan bien trong hoc thuat, Andrew Ng co lien quan den viec danh gia va phan bien cac cong trinh nghien cuu AI trong su nghiep hoc thuat cua minh. Ong tung co vai tro trong cong dong nghien cuu, tap chi va hoi nghi lien quan den AI, machine learning va robotics.

Neu "AI reviewer" nghia la mot san pham, cong cu hoac ung dung co ten nhu vay, hien khong co bang chung ro rang rang "Andrew Ng AI Reviewer" la mot san pham chinh thuc noi tieng cua Andrew Ng.

Vi vay, khi nhac den "Andrew Ng AI reviewer", nen phan biet:

- Andrew Ng la chuyen gia AI co kha nang danh gia, phan tich va phan bien cac van de AI.
- Andrew Ng co dong gop trong hoc thuat va giao duc AI.
- Nhung "AI Reviewer" khong phai la mot ten san pham chinh thuc pho bien gan voi ong.

## 11. Tu duy AI noi bat cua Andrew Ng

Andrew Ng co mot so quan diem rat noi bat ve AI.

### AI giong nhu dien

Ong tung so sanh AI voi dien. Y tuong la AI co the tro thanh mot cong nghe nen tang, anh huong den nhieu nganh khac nhau, giong nhu cach dien da thay doi cong nghiep, giao thong, san xuat va doi song.

### Du lieu rat quan trong

Andrew Ng nhan manh rang nhieu du an AI that bai khong phai vi thieu thuat toan phuc tap, ma vi du lieu kem chat luong, bai toan khong ro, hoac quy trinh danh gia khong dung.

### Chon dung bai toan quan trong hon chay theo hype

Theo tu duy cua ong, mot du an AI nen bat dau tu cau hoi:

- Bai toan can giai quyet la gi?
- Co du lieu phu hop khong?
- Ket qua duoc do luong bang cach nao?
- AI co that su tao gia tri hon cach lam hien tai khong?

### AI nen duoc pho cap

Andrew Ng tin rang AI khong chi danh cho nha nghien cuu. Nha quan ly, nha sang lap, ky su, sinh vien va nguoi lam trong nhieu linh vuc deu nen hieu AI o muc phu hop voi cong viec cua minh.

## 12. Diem manh cua Andrew Ng

Andrew Ng co nhieu diem manh noi bat:

- Giai thich kien thuc kho mot cach de hieu.
- Co nen tang nghien cuu vung chac.
- Co kinh nghiem xay dung AI trong cong ty lon.
- Co kha nang bien kien thuc AI thanh chuong trinh hoc co he thong.
- Co tu duy thuc dung ve ung dung AI trong doanh nghiep.
- Co anh huong lon den cong dong nguoi hoc AI toan cau.

Noi ngan gon, Andrew Ng la cau noi giua nghien cuu, giao duc va ung dung AI.

## 13. Nhung diem co the bi phe binh

Can nhin Andrew Ng mot cach can bang. Mot so y kien cho rang:

- Mot so khoa hoc cua ong co tinh nen tang, nen co the cham voi nguoi muon di thang vao large language models hoac generative AI.
- Cac bai tap trong khoa hoc thuong duoc thiet ke gon gang, khac voi moi truong production that.
- Ong co xu huong lac quan va thuc dung ve AI ung dung, trong khi mot so nha nghien cuu khac tap trung nhieu hon vao rui ro dai han cua AI.

Tuy nhien, cac diem nay khong lam giam gia tri cua Andrew Ng. No chi cho thay ong dai dien cho mot goc nhin cu the: hoc nen tang, xay ung dung, cai thien du lieu va dua AI vao thuc te.

## 14. So sanh voi mot so nhan vat AI khac

- Geoffrey Hinton: noi bat ve nen tang neural networks va deep learning.
- Yann LeCun: noi bat ve computer vision, convolutional neural networks va AI tai Meta.
- Yoshua Bengio: noi bat ve deep learning, representation learning va gan day quan tam nhieu den AI safety.
- Fei-Fei Li: noi bat ve computer vision, ImageNet va human-centered AI.
- Demis Hassabis: noi bat voi DeepMind, AlphaGo va AlphaFold.
- Sam Altman: noi bat ve OpenAI, ChatGPT va chien luoc san pham generative AI.
- Andrew Ng: noi bat ve giao duc AI, Google Brain, Coursera, DeepLearning.AI, data-centric AI va ung dung AI trong doanh nghiep.

Trong ban do AI, Andrew Ng la mot nha giao duc va nha ung dung AI hang dau, dong thoi co nen tang nghien cuu rat manh.

## 15. Nen hoc gi tu Andrew Ng?

Tu Andrew Ng, co the rut ra mot so bai hoc quan trong:

1. Nen hoc nen tang machine learning truoc khi chay theo cong cu moi.
2. AI can duoc gan voi bai toan thuc te.
3. Du lieu tot co the quan trong ngang hoac hon model tot.
4. Nguoi khong chuyen ky thuat van nen hieu AI o muc chien luoc.
5. Hoc AI hieu qua nhat khi ket hop ly thuyet voi project thuc hanh.
6. Khong nen xem AI la phep mau; can co quy trinh danh gia, thu nghiem va cai tien.

## 17. Danh gia tong quan

Andrew Ng la mot nhan vat rat dang tim hieu neu muon hieu AI hien dai. Ong khong phai nguoi phat minh ra AI, va cung khong phai la nguoi duy nhat tao nen deep learning. Tuy nhien, ong co vai tro rat lon trong viec dua AI den voi nhieu nguoi hon.

Anh huong cua Andrew Ng nam o ba truc lon:

- Nghien cuu va cong nghiep: Google Brain, Baidu, Landing AI.
- Giao duc: Coursera, DeepLearning.AI, cac khoa hoc machine learning va deep learning.
- Tu duy ung dung: data-centric AI, AI cho doanh nghiep, AI nhu mot cong nghe nen tang.

Neu can tom tat bang mot cau: Andrew Ng la mot trong nhung nguoi quan trong nhat trong viec bien AI tu linh vuc kho tiep can thanh mot ky nang va cong nghe ma hang trieu nguoi co the hoc, hieu va ung dung.
